# Search and limitations

**Title:** Multidisciplinary Professional Identity in AI Retrieval: A Bounded Review of Relevant Prior Art  
**Author:** Joseph Ribal Nasr  
**Public version:** 1

This is a bounded, non-systematic, non-exhaustive, AI-assisted narrative review, not a systematic scoping review or experimental MIRF study. The corpus was assembled from three inherited sets of 15 structured records, totaling **45 bibliographic records**. Raw provider exports and complete search-result denominators were not retained. Screening and synthesis were AI-assisted and not independently adjudicated by a human reviewer. This document does not reconstruct unrecorded queries or imply systematic coverage.

The journal DOI **10.1038/s41597-020-0543-2** and arXiv preprint **2005.04308** describe *Building a PubMed Knowledge Graph* and are separate bibliographic rows assigned to one provisional cluster: **45 records → 44 provisional study clusters**. Other duplicate versions cannot be excluded.

Nine references were passage-checked for narrow technical statements, with the cited section locators given in the manuscript. The other 36 records were originally collected and provisionally classified on title/abstract evidence; some also have inherited partial source reviews or pointers, so **“title-abstract only” in the evidence table denotes the verification level credited for this publication, not a claim that none was ever read further**. No source is treated as an independently replicated experiment.

The nine passage-checked studies concern distinct applications (bitemporal representation, graph/identity curation, generated-answer attribution, scholarly publication histories, author-alias disambiguation). They do not demonstrate simultaneous cross-profession role modeling, a novel MIRF implementation, or accuracy improvements. The proposed integration remains untested. This review does not prove an originality gap, and independent human review was not performed.
